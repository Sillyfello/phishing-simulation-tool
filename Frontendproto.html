Understanding Phishing Attacks

Phishing is a deceptive cyberattack where criminals pose as trusted entities (banks, colleagues, HR, cloud services) to trick people into revealing sensitive data such as passwords, credit card numbers, or personal information.

Common Types of Phishing

  • Email phishing: Mass emails imitating legitimate brands or services.
  • Spear phishing: Targeted messages tailored to an individual or team.
  • Smishing: Fraudulent SMS/text messages with malicious links.
  • Vishing: Phone calls impersonating help desks or banks.
  • Clone phishing: Real email copied and resent with a malicious link/attachment.
  • Business Email Compromise (BEC): Impersonation of executives or vendors to request payments or data.

How It Works

Attackers exploit urgency (e.g., “account locked”, “invoice overdue”), curiosity, or authority to make you click a link, open an attachment, or enter credentials on a fake site that looks legitimate.

Potential Harms

  • Financial loss (fraudulent transfers, gift cards, stolen card details)
  • Identity theft and long-term account takeover
  • Malware/ransomware infections and data breaches
  • Reputational damage for individuals and organizations

How to Protect Yourself

  • Hover over links to inspect the real URL before clicking.
  • Verify unexpected requests via a separate channel (call the sender).
  • Use multi-factor authentication (MFA) on important accounts.
  • Keep browsers and antivirus up to date; report suspicious emails.
